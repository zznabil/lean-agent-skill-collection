## Boundaries

- `practice-versioned-verification-requirements` does not duplicate the broader `standard-owasp-asvs` routine in open PR #17.
- `practice-implementation-example-traceability` does not duplicate the broader `standard-nist-ssdf` routine in open PR #17.
- The Microsoft and GitHub profiles render procedures after technical correctness is established.
- ANSI and ISO source texts are not bundled. Obtain authorised copies for clause-level or conformity work.
- OSHA, WHO, FDA, NASA and military-source mechanisms do not transfer their legal or organisational authority to unrelated tasks.
