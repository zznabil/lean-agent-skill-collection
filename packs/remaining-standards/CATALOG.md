## Read the source notes before asserting coverage
Every routine links its own SOURCES.md. Nineteen ISO/IEC/IEEE entries lack licensed full text; their routines are explicitly source-gated. Series entries still require a deliberate part/edition choice. Native HTML, schemas and research papers are labelled by their real type.

## Selection examples
Use OpenAPI for an HTTP interface description, AsyncAPI for message-driven operations, and CloudEvents for the event envelope; they are not interchangeable. Use an assurance case for a claim and its evidence, a quality model for selecting attributes, and a review method for inspecting a work product.

Use watched/deferred/rejected entries only to handle their explicit governance/selection question. Do not add them to the ordinary skill search path as universal validators.
