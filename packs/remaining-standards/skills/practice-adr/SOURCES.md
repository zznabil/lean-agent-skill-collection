## What the routine represents
The routine covers ADR organisation definitions and practices. ADR is a practice, not a formal numbered standard.

SKILL.md provides a new Lean application and verification procedure in independent wording. Lean policy defines its task boundaries, evidence safeguards and bounded correction pass. These rules are not source quotations or additional publisher clauses.
The routine does not include every source clause, definition or example. Source selection, source access, clause coverage and actual execution are separate concepts. A full assessment requires the complete applicable source and evidence.
