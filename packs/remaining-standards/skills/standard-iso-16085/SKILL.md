## Procedure
1. Confirm the source identity before reading clauses: the historical register URL resolves to a different standard.
2. Use the corrected official source for ISO/IEC/IEEE 16085:2021; obtain its authorised full text before a formal assessment.
3. Define the lifecycle objective, risk-management scope, responsibilities and stakeholder reporting needs.
4. Record risks as causes or conditions, uncertain events and consequences for the project or product.
5. Give each risk an owner, current controls, assessment basis and decision or escalation criterion.
6. Analyse and prioritise using project criteria without turning guesses into measured probabilities.
7. Link selected treatments to implementation, verification and the responsible lifecycle activity.
8. Reassess risks after requirement, design, supplier, environment or operating-condition changes.
9. Keep accepted residual risks and treatment status visible at handoff and transition decisions.
10. Use the licensed process requirements when available; the steps here are Lean's lifecycle-risk application, not recovered
    clauses.
