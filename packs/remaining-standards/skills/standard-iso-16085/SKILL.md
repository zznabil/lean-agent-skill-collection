## Procedure
1. Confirm source identity before reading clauses. The historical register URL resolves to a different standard.
2. Use the corrected official source for ISO/IEC/IEEE 16085:2021. Obtain its authorised full text before a formal assessment.
3. Define the lifecycle objective, risk-management scope, responsibilities and stakeholder reporting needs.
4. Record each risk's causes or conditions, uncertain events and consequences for the project or product.
5. Assign each risk an owner. Record its current controls, assessment basis and decision or escalation criterion.
6. Analyse and prioritise risks with project criteria. Do not present guesses as measured probabilities.
7. Link selected treatments to implementation, verification and the responsible lifecycle activity.
8. Reassess risks after changes to requirements, design, suppliers, environments or operating conditions.
9. Keep accepted residual risks and treatment status visible at handoff and transition decisions.
10. Use the licensed process requirements when available. These steps apply Lean lifecycle-risk practice; they do not reconstruct clauses.
