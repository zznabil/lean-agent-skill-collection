## Procedure
1. State the objective, context, stakeholders, scope and agreed risk criteria.
2. Identify sources of uncertainty, events and potential consequences for the objective.
3. Analyse consequences and likelihood with available evidence. Distinguish estimates from measurements.
4. Account for existing controls and uncertainty about their effectiveness.
5. Evaluate risk against the agreed criteria. Do not use a universal colour matrix invented by the agent.
6. Identify treatment options, the responsible owner, required resources and residual risks.
7. Record the authorised risk decision. Acceptance of residual risk requires the appropriate authority.
8. Communicate relevant assumptions. Obtain consultation where the decision affects others.
9. Set monitoring indicators, change triggers and review timing in proportion to the risk.
10. Use the full standard for a formal process assessment. This routine covers scoped Lean risk work.

## Verify and recover
- **Worked check (illustrative, not executed):** No incidents occurred in a short test, so a risk is assigned zero probability.
- **Expected:** Record the limited observation and remaining uncertainty. Do not declare the risk impossible.
- **If blocked:** If risk criteria or authority to accept residual risk is missing, stop the affected assessment or action. Record the missing prerequisite. Request it from the responsible owner. Continue only independent, authorised work.
- Preserve facts, identifiers, links, required checks, permissions, negation, exceptions and failure/recovery paths.
- Distinguish planned work, actual evidence and unknown results. Missing or stale evidence is not a pass.
- Do not delete requirements to improve a score or meet the line budget. Record unresolved source or task conflicts.
- Use the smallest check that can detect the relevant defect. A schema, linter or inventory alone does not prove task success.
