## Procedure
1. Confirm the AI system, intended use, lifecycle stage, jurisdictional context and assessment owner.
2. Obtain the applicable authoritative edition before assigning clauses or claiming a complete ISO assessment.
3. Identify people and groups that the system affects directly or indirectly. Include non-users.
4. Describe foreseeable beneficial and adverse impacts. Use evidence and clearly label assumptions.
5. Consider impact severity, likelihood, distribution and context. Use criteria that the responsible organisation sets.
6. Record data/model dependencies, deployment constraints and uncertainty that can change the assessment.
7. Consult relevant stakeholders when required. Do not invent completed consultation.
8. Identify mitigations, accountable owners, residual impacts and escalation or approval decisions.
9. Set review triggers for material changes or newly observed impacts.
10. Report scope, evidence and unresolved questions. If the historical catalogue URL cannot be retrieved, verify the source link independently.
