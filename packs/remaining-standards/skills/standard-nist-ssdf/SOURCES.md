## What the routine represents
The routine covers the PO, PS, PW and RV practice/task table in NIST SP 800-218 version 1.1. The examples illustrate the practices and tasks.

SKILL.md provides a new Lean procedure for application and verification. It uses independent wording. Lean policy defines its task boundaries, evidence safeguards and single correction pass. These rules are not publisher quotations or additional publisher clauses.
The routine does not include every source clause, definition or example. Treat source selection, source access, clause coverage and execution as separate matters. A full assessment requires the complete applicable source and evidence.
