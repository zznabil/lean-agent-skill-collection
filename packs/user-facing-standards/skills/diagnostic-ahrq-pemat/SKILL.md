# AHRQ PEMAT: understandability and actionability

## Task and result
- Use this skill for patient education material in PEMAT-P or PEMAT-A/V scope. Deliver separate understandability and actionability findings.
- Select the form for the actual medium. This communication review does not assess diagnosis, treatment safety or clinical completeness.

## Source and limits
- Read the official PEMAT and User’s Guide (2013, updated 2014) and the selected form linked in SOURCES.md.
- The official 67-page PDF contains instructions and forms. Binary retrieval received HTTP 403. The pack therefore links it but does not bundle it.
- If the actual material or applicable form is unavailable, mark the assessment incomplete. Do not invent a score.

## Assessment procedure
1. Read or view the complete material once. Understand its purpose before you rate individual items.
2. Review the guide’s explanations for the selected form. Rate the material itself, not what you already know.
3. Assess understandability through purpose, content, words, numbers, organisation, layout and relevant visual aids.
4. Assess actionability through explicit actions, direct address, manageable steps and the supports needed to act.
5. Apply the selected form’s criteria to calculations, tools and visuals. Do not assume that all examples apply.
6. Record Agree as 1 and Disagree as 0. Include evidence. Use N/A only when the item explicitly permits it.
7. Calculate understandability and actionability percentages separately. Use each scale’s applicable-item denominator.
8. Handle a missing applicable feature as the form specifies. Do not use N/A merely to avoid a low score.
9. Flag ambiguous ratings. After an edit, inspect the full changed material. Rescore applicable items on each separate scale.

## Task requirements
- Do not combine the two scales into an invented overall PEMAT percentage.
- This skill introduces no universal 70%, 80% or other release threshold.
- Understandability/actionability ratings do not prove actual comprehension, clinical accuracy or treatment effectiveness.
- For material changes, retain the qualified clinical review and intended-patient testing that the task requires.

## Verification and delivery
- Report the form, material revision, both scores, denominators, item evidence, excluded items and limitations.
- Keep factual/clinical defects visible even if the communication score is high.
- Return scoped findings or an authorised communication revision. Never invent clinical instructions to improve actionability.

## Worked distinction
The handout is easy to read but gives no concrete steps for the recommended action.
Keep the scores separate. Identify the missing action support. Do not fabricate clinical advice.
