# AHRQ PEMAT: understandability and actionability

## Use
- Use for patient education material in PEMAT-P or PEMAT-A/V scope. Deliver separate understandability and actionability findings.
- Choose the form for the actual medium. This communication review does not assess diagnosis, treatment safety or clinical completeness.

## Source and scope
- Read the official PEMAT and User’s Guide (2013, updated 2014) and the selected form linked in SOURCES.md.
- The official 67-page PDF includes instructions and forms. Binary retrieval received HTTP 403, so it is linked
  only.
- If the actual material or applicable form is unavailable, mark the assessment incomplete rather than invent a
  score.

## Procedure
1. Read or view the complete material once to understand its purpose before rating individual items.
2. Review the guide’s explanations for the selected form and rate the material itself, not what you already know.
3. Assess understandability through purpose, content, words, numbers, organisation, layout and relevant visual aids.
4. Assess actionability through explicit actions, direct address, manageable steps and the supports needed to act.
5. Apply the selected form’s criteria to calculations, tools and visuals instead of assuming that all examples are
  applicable.
6. Record Agree as 1 and Disagree as 0, with evidence. Use N/A only for an item that explicitly permits it.
7. Compute understandability and actionability percentages separately using each scale’s applicable-item
  denominator.
8. Treat a missing applicable feature as the form specifies; do not use N/A merely to avoid a low score.
9. Flag ambiguous ratings. After an edit, inspect the full changed material and rescore applicable items on each separate scale.

## Keep the task contract
- Do not combine the two scales into an invented overall PEMAT percentage.
- No universal 70%, 80% or other release threshold is introduced by this skill.
- Understandability/actionability ratings do not prove actual comprehension, clinical accuracy or treatment
  effectiveness.
- For material changes, retain qualified clinical review and intended-patient testing required by the task.

## Verify and deliver
- Report form, material revision, both scores, denominators, item evidence, excluded items and limitations.
- Keep factual/clinical defects visible even when the communication score is high.
- Return scoped findings or an authorised communication revision; never invent clinical instructions to improve
  actionability.

## Worked distinction
The handout is easy to read but gives no concrete steps for the recommended action.
Keep the two scores separate and identify the missing action support without fabricating clinical advice.

## Lean communication kernel (standalone fallback)
If root `AGENTS.md` is loaded, it governs. Otherwise apply these rules to communication. This skill’s source-specific procedure runs only for its task, not every reply.
- Lead with the supported result and next action. Use short, active ASD-STE100-inspired technical wording and CDC-style familiar words. Keep how-to, reference and explanation apart when Diátaxis separation helps.
- Preserve facts, exact negation, actors, conditions, exceptions, rights, permissions, uncertainty, evidence and requested format. Never call an unchecked result compliant or complete.
- In normative text, keep BCP 14 MUST/SHOULD/MAY force and exceptions. For important requirements, name one actor, action and observable check (NASA).
- Before a hazardous action, show the verified risk and an ANSI-style warning. For critical steps, use a WHO-style hold point and OSHA-style safe-state check; give the FDA-style expected result, failure sign and recovery when failure is plausible. These analogies do not replace task-specific controls.
- For measurable multi-step work, use a named 20-cell ASCII bar (# processed, - remaining) and floor percentage from durable counts; keep the PASS/FAIL/BLOCKED verdict separate. A failed, blocked, skipped or untested item counts only when classified with evidence. With no defensible total, report phase, evidence and next action without a bar. This does not invoke manual wait-what.
- Explain a difficult mechanism from foundations (Feynman). Use SEI CERT-style compliant/noncompliant contrast for code or configuration only when useful. Do not force examples or sections on simple tasks.
