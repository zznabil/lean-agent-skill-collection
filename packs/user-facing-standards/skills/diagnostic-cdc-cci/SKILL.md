# CDC Clear Communication Index: scoped diagnosis

## Use
- Use to assess defined public information with the CDC Clear Communication Index. Deliver item evidence and a score only when the applicable form and material were checked.
- Identify audience, health-literacy context, objective and main message first. Do not turn this diagnostic into a universal release gate.

## Source and scope
- Read the official user guide and appropriate full or modified score sheet linked in SOURCES.md.
- The PDFs were accessible for web review but the binary downloader received HTTP 403; no PDF is bundled.
- If the form or actual material cannot be inspected, give a qualitative review and identify why a score is
  incomplete.

## Procedure
1. Read the whole material in its delivered form, including graphics, headings, numbers and visible links.
2. Record the four introductory answers and select the score sheet that fits the material’s format.
3. Assess the main message and call to action, their placement and supporting visual emphasis.
4. Assess familiar language, active main-message/action wording, information chunks and meaningful headings.
5. Assess whether the material represents the state of the science, including what remains uncertain.
6. Apply the relevant behavioural-recommendation, number and risk items using their actual wording and skip logic.
7. For each applicable item, record the observed passage or design evidence and the required zero-or-one rating.
8. Calculate the percentage from the applicable-item denominator. Show ratings and unresolved evidence so the result can be checked.
9. Revise evidence-backed defects and rescore the changed material; do not remove necessary facts to increase the
  score.

## Keep the task contract
- The official tool has 20 scored items in seven areas; applicability comes from the score sheet, not convenience.
- CDC describes 90 or higher as passing within this tool. That is not a universal Lean acceptance threshold.
- A score does not replace factual checking, formative research or pretesting with the intended audience.
- Do not infer clinical accuracy, behaviour change or WCAG conformance from a communication score.

## Verify and deliver
- Report the form/version, audience, ratings, calculation, diagnostic issues and actual review limits.
- Separate a passing index score from any unmet task, safety or evidence requirement.
- Return the useful findings or revised material first, with the score only when it was actually calculated.

## Worked distinction
A clear headline scores well, but the body omits what the reader should do next.
Evaluate the call-to-action items and the real task; the headline alone is not acceptance evidence.

## Lean communication kernel (standalone fallback)
If root `AGENTS.md` is loaded, it governs. Otherwise apply these rules to communication. This skill’s source-specific procedure runs only for its task, not every reply.
- Lead with the supported result and next action. Use short, active ASD-STE100-inspired technical wording and CDC-style familiar words. Keep how-to, reference and explanation apart when Diátaxis separation helps.
- Preserve facts, exact negation, actors, conditions, exceptions, rights, permissions, uncertainty, evidence and requested format. Never call an unchecked result compliant or complete.
- In normative text, keep BCP 14 MUST/SHOULD/MAY force and exceptions. For important requirements, name one actor, action and observable check (NASA).
- Before a hazardous action, show the verified risk and an ANSI-style warning. For critical steps, use a WHO-style hold point and OSHA-style safe-state check; give the FDA-style expected result, failure sign and recovery when failure is plausible. These analogies do not replace task-specific controls.
- For measurable multi-step work, use a named 20-cell ASCII bar (# processed, - remaining) and floor percentage from durable counts; keep the PASS/FAIL/BLOCKED verdict separate. A failed, blocked, skipped or untested item counts only when classified with evidence. With no defensible total, report phase, evidence and next action without a bar. This does not invoke manual wait-what.
- Explain a difficult mechanism from foundations (Feynman). Use SEI CERT-style compliant/noncompliant contrast for code or configuration only when useful. Do not force examples or sections on simple tasks.
