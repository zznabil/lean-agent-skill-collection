# CAST UDL 3.0: remove learning barriers

## Use
- Use to design a learning task, resource or assessment for varied learners. Deliver options tied to a learning goal and observed barriers.
- State the goal and barriers first. Do not apply every option to every learner or task.

## Source and scope
- Read the official 2024 UDL 3.0 graphic organiser linked in [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).
- It is an official one-page overview, not the complete online explanations for all considerations.
- Use the official detailed consideration when its interpretation matters; state when only the overview was checked.

## Procedure
1. Separate the intended learning goal from an unnecessary constraint on how learners receive information or
  respond.
2. For engagement, consider interests and identities, meaningful goals, sustained effort, belonging and emotional
  capacity.
3. Offer relevant choice, manageable challenge and actionable feedback; do not replace the goal with entertainment.
4. For representation, inspect access to perception, language and symbols, and the connections needed to build
  knowledge.
5. Clarify unfamiliar vocabulary and notation; connect prior knowledge and concrete examples to the new concept.
6. For action and expression, inspect access to interaction, communication tools and strategy development.
7. Allow alternative ways to demonstrate the same learning goal when a fixed response method is an avoidable
  barrier.
8. Support planning, organising resources and monitoring progress where learners need those supports.
9. Identify bias or exclusion in the material, expectations and available participation routes.
10. Observe participation and learning for the chosen options. Revise barriers that persist; choice alone does not show that the goal was met.

## Keep the task contract
- Do not infer ability, motivation or support needs from a diagnosis alone.
- Do not lower a required learning objective merely to make an assessment easier.
- Do not force multiple representations, a quiz or extra choices when they add no useful access.
- Applying an idea to agent-facing instructions is an adaptation, not evidence that agents learn like people.

## Verify and deliver
- Map each chosen option to a barrier, a UDL consideration and observable evidence.
- Check access and learning separately; liking a resource does not establish mastery.
- Return the learning design and material unresolved barriers, not a claim of universal accessibility.

## Worked distinction
An assessment of causal reasoning unnecessarily requires rapid handwriting.
Consider another response method while preserving the causal-reasoning objective and its evidence requirements.

## Lean communication kernel (standalone fallback)
If root `AGENTS.md` is loaded, it governs. Otherwise apply these rules to communication. This skill’s source-specific procedure runs only for its task, not every reply.
- Lead with the supported result and next action. Use short, active ASD-STE100-inspired technical wording and CDC-style familiar words. Keep how-to, reference and explanation apart when Diátaxis separation helps.
- Preserve facts, exact negation, actors, conditions, exceptions, rights, permissions, uncertainty, evidence and requested format. Never call an unchecked result compliant or complete.
- In normative text, keep BCP 14 MUST/SHOULD/MAY force and exceptions. For important requirements, name one actor, action and observable check (NASA).
- Before a hazardous action, show the verified risk and an ANSI-style warning. For critical steps, use a WHO-style hold point and OSHA-style safe-state check; give the FDA-style expected result, failure sign and recovery when failure is plausible. These analogies do not replace task-specific controls.
- For measurable multi-step work, use a named 20-cell ASCII bar (# processed, - remaining) and floor percentage from durable counts; keep the PASS/FAIL/BLOCKED verdict separate. A failed, blocked, skipped or untested item counts only when classified with evidence. With no defensible total, report phase, evidence and next action without a bar. This does not invoke manual wait-what.
- Explain a difficult mechanism from foundations (Feynman). Use SEI CERT-style compliant/noncompliant contrast for code or configuration only when useful. Do not force examples or sections on simple tasks.
