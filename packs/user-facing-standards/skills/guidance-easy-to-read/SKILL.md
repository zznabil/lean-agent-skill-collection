# Inclusion Europe: Easy-to-Read authoring

## Use
- Use when Easy-to-Read is requested or justified by intended readers and task. Deliver reviewed material or a draft explicitly awaiting intended-user review.
- Treat Easy-to-Read as a specialised accessible representation, not merely short text or general plain language.

## Source and scope
- Use Inclusion Europe’s official Information for all booklet and current rules/logo conditions linked in
  SOURCES.md.
- No original PDF is bundled because permission to redistribute it in this pack was not established.
- Intended-user involvement is part of the method; an agent-only wording pass cannot substitute for it.

## Procedure
1. Identify the intended readers, what they need to know and what they must be able to do.
2. Involve people with relevant intellectual disabilities in planning, drafting or checking the information as the
  method requires.
3. Choose familiar concrete words, explain necessary difficult terms and use the same term for the same thing.
4. Express one idea at a time, in short clear sentences and a logical order; make the actor and action visible.
5. Use direct literal statements. Prefer simpler constructions without changing a prohibition, exception or
  uncertainty.
6. Use relevant pictures or symbols that support the actual meaning and are understandable to the intended readers.
7. Make layout easy to follow with clear type, consistent alignment and spacing; apply the booklet’s medium-specific
  rules.
8. Explain necessary numbers, dates and abbreviations; retain precise values when they determine safe or correct
  action.
9. Ask intended readers to explain and use the material. Revise what they misunderstand and repeat the affected task check.
10. Check current attribution, proofreading and logo conditions before making an Easy-to-Read claim or adding the
  logo.

## Keep the task contract
- Do not infantilise adult readers, infer identical needs or replace the full version without preserving necessary
  access.
- Do not convert print font sizes directly into arbitrary screen-pixel requirements.
- Do not remove a critical quantity merely because a vague phrase is easier to read.
- Never add the European Easy-to-Read logo or claim intended-user approval without the required evidence.

## Verify and deliver
- Compare meaning with the source, including the action, warning, numerical limit and exception.
- Without intended-user review, label the result an Easy-to-Read draft for review.
- Report material source gaps, review status and any unmet logo conditions separately from writing quality.

## Worked distinction
A safety instruction requires a precise waiting time.
Explain that time clearly; do not replace it with “wait a little” to shorten the sentence.

## Lean communication kernel (standalone fallback)
If root `AGENTS.md` is loaded, it governs. Otherwise apply these rules to communication. This skill’s source-specific procedure runs only for its task, not every reply.
- Lead with the supported result and next action. Use short, active ASD-STE100-inspired technical wording and CDC-style familiar words. Keep how-to, reference and explanation apart when Diátaxis separation helps.
- Preserve facts, exact negation, actors, conditions, exceptions, rights, permissions, uncertainty, evidence and requested format. Never call an unchecked result compliant or complete.
- In normative text, keep BCP 14 MUST/SHOULD/MAY force and exceptions. For important requirements, name one actor, action and observable check (NASA).
- Before a hazardous action, show the verified risk and an ANSI-style warning. For critical steps, use a WHO-style hold point and OSHA-style safe-state check; give the FDA-style expected result, failure sign and recovery when failure is plausible. These analogies do not replace task-specific controls.
- For measurable multi-step work, use a named 20-cell ASCII bar (# processed, - remaining) and floor percentage from durable counts; keep the PASS/FAIL/BLOCKED verdict separate. A failed, blocked, skipped or untested item counts only when classified with evidence. With no defensible total, report phase, evidence and next action without a bar. This does not invoke manual wait-what.
- Explain a difficult mechanism from foundations (Feynman). Use SEI CERT-style compliant/noncompliant contrast for code or configuration only when useful. Do not force examples or sections on simple tasks.
