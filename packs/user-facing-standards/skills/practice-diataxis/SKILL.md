# Diátaxis: documentation by reader need

## Use
- Use when substantial documentation mixes purposes or makes the reader’s task hard to find. Deliver a usable document or bounded reorganisation.
- Choose the primary reader need; do not force four documents or a website taxonomy onto a short answer.

## Source and scope
- The official primer and four form guides are bundled as HTML text in references/.
- Diátaxis has four forms: tutorial, how-to guide, reference and explanation. A decision brief is not a fifth form.

## Procedure
1. Identify whether the reader needs supported learning, a practical solution, exact facts or conceptual
  understanding.
2. For a tutorial, guide a newcomer through a dependable learning experience with visible progress and working
  results.
3. Keep tutorial choices and theory subordinate to successful learning; provide only what is needed at each step.
4. For a how-to guide, assume relevant competence and solve the named real-world task; make conditions and branches
  explicit.
5. Do not turn a how-to guide into a lesson on basic concepts the intended reader already understands.
6. For reference, organise exact factual information to mirror the system, using consistent structure and complete
  detail.
7. Do not hide exceptions, defaults, limits or field meanings in unrelated narrative when the reader needs a lookup.
8. For explanation, develop context, mechanisms, reasons, alternatives and connections that build understanding.
9. If mixed purposes obstruct the task, move the secondary material to its own form and link to it where needed. Keep the primary path intact.
10. Improve one evidence-backed documentation problem at a time; do not force a large reorganisation without need.

## Keep the task contract
- Preserve required procedures, technical distinctions, safety information and working links when moving content.
- A tutorial example is not a substitute for complete reference coverage.
- Do not delete a useful section merely because it does not fit a proposed navigation label.

## Verify and deliver
- Test the document against its primary reader question and verify any commands or factual reference entries.
- Inspect links across forms and ensure a required instruction remains reachable before its use.
- Return the revised document or a bounded structure proposal; state any unverified execution example.

## Worked distinction
“How do I restore a backup?” needs a how-to path with conditions and recovery.
“Why does recovery use a log?” needs explanation. Link them without mixing their primary jobs.

## Lean communication kernel (standalone fallback)
If root `AGENTS.md` is loaded, it governs. Otherwise apply these rules to communication. This skill’s source-specific procedure runs only for its task, not every reply.
- Lead with the supported result and next action. Use short, active ASD-STE100-inspired technical wording and CDC-style familiar words. Keep how-to, reference and explanation apart when Diátaxis separation helps.
- Preserve facts, exact negation, actors, conditions, exceptions, rights, permissions, uncertainty, evidence and requested format. Never call an unchecked result compliant or complete.
- In normative text, keep BCP 14 MUST/SHOULD/MAY force and exceptions. For important requirements, name one actor, action and observable check (NASA).
- Before a hazardous action, show the verified risk and an ANSI-style warning. For critical steps, use a WHO-style hold point and OSHA-style safe-state check; give the FDA-style expected result, failure sign and recovery when failure is plausible. These analogies do not replace task-specific controls.
- For measurable multi-step work, use a named 20-cell ASCII bar (# processed, - remaining) and floor percentage from durable counts; keep the PASS/FAIL/BLOCKED verdict separate. A failed, blocked, skipped or untested item counts only when classified with evidence. With no defensible total, report phase, evidence and next action without a bar. This does not invoke manual wait-what.
- Explain a difficult mechanism from foundations (Feynman). Use SEI CERT-style compliant/noncompliant contrast for code or configuration only when useful. Do not force examples or sections on simple tasks.
