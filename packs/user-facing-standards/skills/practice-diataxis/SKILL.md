# Diátaxis: documentation by reader need

## Use
- Use to create or repair substantial documentation whose purpose or organisation is unclear.
- Select the reader need, not a compulsory website taxonomy. A short answer does not need four documents.

## Source and scope
- The official primer and four form guides are bundled as HTML text in references/.
- Diátaxis has four forms: tutorial, how-to guide, reference and explanation. A decision brief is not a fifth form.

## Procedure
1. Identify whether the reader needs supported learning, a practical solution, exact facts or conceptual
  understanding.
2. For a tutorial, guide a newcomer through a dependable learning experience with visible progress and working
  results.
3. Keep tutorial choices and theory subordinate to successful learning; provide only what is needed at each step.
4. For a how-to guide, assume relevant competence and solve the named real-world task; make conditions and branches
  explicit.
5. Do not turn a how-to guide into a lesson on basic concepts the intended reader already understands.
6. For reference, organise exact factual information to mirror the system, using consistent structure and complete
  detail.
7. Do not hide exceptions, defaults, limits or field meanings in unrelated narrative when the reader needs a lookup.
8. For explanation, develop context, mechanisms, reasons, alternatives and connections that build understanding.
9. Separate different reader needs where mixing them obstructs use; link to the other form at the relevant point.
10. Improve one evidence-backed documentation problem at a time; do not force a large reorganisation without need.

## Preserve the boundary
- Preserve required procedures, technical distinctions, safety information and working links when moving content.
- A tutorial example is not a substitute for complete reference coverage.
- Do not delete a useful section merely because it does not fit a proposed navigation label.

## Check and finish
- Test the document against its primary reader question and verify any commands or factual reference entries.
- Inspect links across forms and ensure a required instruction remains reachable before its use.
- Return the revised document or a bounded structure proposal; state any unverified execution example.

## Worked distinction
“How do I restore a backup?” needs a how-to path with conditions and recovery.
“Why does recovery use a log?” needs explanation. Link them without mixing their primary jobs.
