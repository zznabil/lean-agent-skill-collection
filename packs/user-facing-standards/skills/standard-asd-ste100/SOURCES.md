# ASD-STE100 Issue 9 — source status

The approved 99-line prototype is byte-for-byte unchanged.

Official PDF: https://www.asd-ste100.org/assets/files/ASD-STE100_ISSUE9.pdf
Official access: https://www.asd-ste100.org/STE_downloads.html

The complete PDF includes Part 1 writing rules and Part 2 dictionary. It is freely obtainable from the publisher,
but its copyright notice restricts reproduction without permission. This bundle does not assume permission
from free access. The PDF and dictionary are therefore not redistributed here. Obtain an authorised copy
through the official access page for full vocabulary and rule checks.

The skill summarises a working procedure; it is not the whole standard and does not certify conformance.
Its meaning-preservation and authority safeguards are Lean integration policy. The original Chelebi study
and nine unexecuted acceptance cases are retained in the audit directory, not additional mandatory instructions.
