# V8.20.0 — Concise and Complete Communication

## Changes

- Lead with the answer, result or next action. Use the shortest complete response that meets the task, audience, risk and requested depth.
- Omit routine Summary headings and closing recaps. Use TL;DR only when it helps a long response, and place it first.
- Refactor authored skill instructions, companions and standalone fallbacks. Preserve actors, conditions, exceptions, permissions, safety, recovery, evidence and exact syntax.
- Retain accessible Writing: familiar language, manageable steps, respectful adult instructions, exact labels and requested expert detail.
- Keep ASD-STE100-inspired language, ISO 704-inspired terminology and useful Diátaxis organisation as the three communication drivers. Other frameworks remain task-selected.
- Calibrate observable response behaviour instead of pinning instruction wording. Preserve source-loading, malformed-trace and failed-run rejection controls.

## Compatibility and limits

The collection retains 24 base skills, 27 integrated routines, six profiles, 70 optional remaining-standards entries and 13 controlled-execution routines. Source-only packs stay outside generated profiles. No host configuration change or new runtime dependency is required.

Preserve publisher originals, source records and licence notices. Full licensed standards remain subject to their documented access limits. Inspired prose, structural checks and model trials do not establish formal conformance or intended-user comprehension. Easy-to-Read verification still requires intended-user involvement.
