# Interface directions

Use one as a starting grammar, then adapt it to the product.

- **Product clear:** restrained palette, strong task hierarchy, dense where work demands it, minimal motion, explicit state.
- **Editorial motion:** typographic rhythm, asymmetric composition, large media, controlled scroll or transition motion, quiet controls.
- **Minimal:** few elements, generous space, precise type and alignment, one accent, no decoration without function.
- **Industrial:** exposed grid, hard edges, high contrast, utilitarian labels, deliberate roughness, strong interaction feedback.
- **Cinematic:** layered depth, rich imagery, controlled contrast, selective glow, slow purposeful motion, simple navigation.

A direction is not a theme pack. Preserve accessibility, content hierarchy, platform conventions, and product credibility.
