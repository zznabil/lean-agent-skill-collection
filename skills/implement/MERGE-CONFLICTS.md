# Resolve merge conflicts

Inspect the current operation, base, ours and theirs. Preserve unrelated work and understand both intended changes before resolving. Do not choose one whole side merely because it is shorter or currently passes a narrow test.

Resolve coupled semantics together, including generated files, contracts and dependency locks. Check for remaining markers and verify affected behaviour and integration. Inspect the final diff against both intentions.

Finish the authorised operation and read back its state. Do not force-push or rewrite shared history without explicit approval covering that action. Report the resolution, actual checks and any unresolved conflict rather than claiming a clean merge from absence of markers alone.
