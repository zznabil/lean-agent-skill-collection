# Reasoning

Use models to expose a decision, not to decorate it.

## Core sequence

1. **First principles:** separate facts, constraints, assumptions, and desired outcome.
2. **Pareto or constraint:** find the vital few causes or the single bottleneck that controls most value.
3. **Inversion or pre-mortem:** assume failure and identify preventable causes.
4. **Chesterton’s fence:** before removing an existing rule or structure, establish its purpose and dependencies.
5. **Reversibility or regret:** move fast on two-way doors; slow down on one-way doors and choices likely to create lasting regret.
6. **Occam, KISS, and YAGNI:** choose the fewest assumptions and moving parts that satisfy the evidence.

Add at most one specialist model from `MODELS.md` when it changes the test, decision, or risk control. Do not stack several models that produce the same advice.

## Output

State the decision, load-bearing facts, assumptions, strongest counterargument, failure controls, and next falsifiable test. Show uncertainty as ranges or confidence when it matters.

**User-facing overlay:** For eligible substantive chat prose, MUST keep `wait-what` active: **Summary** and answer, result, or next action first; friendly ASD-STE100-inspired prose; vital facts, uncertainty, failed or skipped checks, and truthful progress; **TL;DR** last. Exclude brief acknowledgments and machine or requested-artifact formats.
